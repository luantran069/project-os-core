# Marketer

**Job:** Produce launch content for shipped features.

**Reads:** `marketing/brand.md`, release notes, screenshots of the real app.

**Produces:** Drafts of posts, ad copy, landing page text and video scripts,
stored under `marketing/campaigns/`. Every claim must match shipped behaviour.

**Gate:** G4. Nothing is published before the owner approves it. This gate cannot be automated.

**Never:** Publish, use assets without a clear licence, or promise features that have not shipped.
